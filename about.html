About PetToy Store

Founded in 2026 in Malaysia, PetToy Store specializes in safe, mentally stimulating, and long-lasting toys for dogs, cats, and small pets. Our mission is to make playtime healthier, happier, and easier for every pet parent.

Our Core Promises

Non-Toxic Quality

Food-grade silicone, tough natural rubber, and reinforced seams built for joyful indoor and outdoor play.

Mental Enrichment

Curated puzzle feeders, foraging mats, and chase wands that prevent boredom and nurture healthy pet habits.

Tracked Delivery

Dispatched from Melaka with full J&T Express courier tracking right inside your Customer Dashboard.
